ARTICLE DOWNLOADER, portable version 8.4
========================================

Runs on any computer with Windows 10 or 11. Nothing to install: Python and everything
else are in this folder. The window language: the «🌐 RU» / «🌐 EN» button at the top.


HOW TO USE
----------
1. Double-click «СКАЧАТЬ СТАТЬИ» (it means «download articles»; or the «Скачать статьи»
   icon on the desktop). On the first start Windows may ask «Run this file?» — click «Run».
   A window with four big buttons opens: the steps of a literature review, in order.
   The yellow «📖 HOW TO START» bar above them explains briefly where to begin and why.
   Step 1 is the red «API» circle in the middle: get the free keys (OpenAlex and Web of
   Science, step by step with pictures; once). When the keys are saved, it turns green.

2. «🔎 Get a DOI list» — if you have no list of articles yet.
   Type keywords (each word or phrase on a new line; synonyms on one line separated by
   «;»), the years, and click «FIND ARTICLES».
   «💡 Suggest words» offers frequent keywords on your topic (free) — click the ones you
   want. «🧹 New topic» clears everything for the next search. Look through the results,
   untick the extra ones and click «Save the list to inbox».
   This search needs Scopus and Web of Science keys: the «API» circle in the middle of the home screen walks you
   through getting them (free, once). Without keys choose «🌐 Through the browser»: you
   click «Export» on the Scopus or Web of Science site and the program takes the file.

2a. «📝 Screen articles» — the program fetches the abstracts. Read and click «✓ Fits»,
   «? Maybe» or «✗ Doesn't fit» (or the keys 1, 2, 3); the next article opens at once.
   Then «Save the selected as a new list».
   With the AI helper, «🤖 Ask the AI about the articles not screened yet» reads the
   abstracts and suggests for every article: fits, questionable or does not fit.
   «🌐 Translate into English» under the abstract translates the title and abstract
   (tick «translate every article at once» to skip the clicks); the translation is kept.
   «🔁 Find more through citations» (lower on the same screen): the program looks at the works
   your «✓ Fits» articles cite and the works citing them and shows the ones linked to them most
   often — what the keyword search missed. The ticked ones go to screening marked «🔁 found
   through citations»; PRISMA counts them on a line of their own.
   The red «⚠ Retracted» mark: the journal withdrew the article; do not cite it.

3. «⬇ Download articles» — the inbox folder opens: choose a file with a DOI list.
   The program opens the database sites in the downloader's browser. If a site asks you
   to pass a check («I am not a robot»), pass it. Then you can leave: downloading starts
   by itself.

4. «📚 Reference list» — a list in the Russian GOST R 7.0.100-2018 style or a journal
   style: APA 7, Elsevier Harvard, Vancouver, ACS, IEEE, Chicago, Nature — or the style of
   any of about 11,000 journals («🔎 A particular journal's style…»: type the journal title;
   doi.org does the formatting, needs the internet). Copy it (italics stay in Word) or save
   it for Word (.docx) or as text (.txt). Also files for Zotero / EndNote / Mendeley (.ris)
   and LaTeX (.bib). «📊 Table of the articles» (with the AI helper): the AI reads the
   downloaded PDFs (or the abstracts when there is no PDF) and fills the columns — object,
   method, conditions, results; you can change them; saved for Excel (.xlsx).
   «🔍 Search the text of the articles»: write what you are looking for (for example,
   ultrasound 40 kHz) and the program shows which downloaded PDFs say it and on which page;
   the «📄 p. N» button opens the PDF at that page. No AI, free.
   «📝 Review outline» (with the AI helper): the AI proposes the sections of the review,
   sorts the articles into them and says what the literature lacks; copy it into Word or save .docx.
   A PRISMA flow diagram
   with numbers (as a picture — for the «Materials and methods» section). The files are
   in the «Список литературы» folder.

PDFs go to the «pdf» folder; the results are written to «Отчёт.csv» (opens in Excel).
If you closed the program before the list was finished, the home screen shows
«▶ Continue». «🧹 Clear all» stops downloading and clears the search and the queue in one
click (downloaded PDFs and lists in inbox stay). «Exit» closes the program.
You can also drop a DOI list file onto the icon: downloading starts at once.


AI HELPER (OPTIONAL)
--------------------
The «🤖 AI helper» bar under the four buttons: red — not connected, green — connected.
It works on your own key of one of five services (tabs in the helper window): Perplexity,
ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), GigaChat (Sber) or YandexGPT (Yandex). OpenAI and
Anthropic do not serve users in Russia; GigaChat and YandexGPT take Russian cards.
What it does:
  • «Topic in your own words» in «🔎 Get a DOI list»: describe the topic as you would to
    a friend, the AI picks English terms and synonyms, the program builds the queries;
  • «🤖 AI pre-screening» in «📝 Screen articles»: the AI reads the abstracts and says
    whether each article fits, with a reason. You still decide; «✓ Accept the AI advice»
    turns the advice into marks, and you can change any of them.
To connect: click the bar, choose the service tab and follow the steps with
screenshots (sign up → top up the API balance → create a key → paste it into the program
→ «Check the key»). Picking words costs less than a cent, pre-screening a hundred articles
from a few cents; the money goes to the service, not to the author. Only the topic and the
titles with abstracts go to the service; the key stays on this computer.


FIRST START ON A NEW COMPUTER: INSTITUTIONAL SIGN-IN
---------------------------------------------------
Publishers let you into paid articles only when they know you are from a university.
Once on every computer:
  the «🏛 Institutional sign-in» button → click a database (ScienceDirect, Springer,
  Wiley, ACS…) → choose your university on the page that opens → sign in as you would
  to your university account.
The sign-in is remembered on this computer. Sometimes a site asks you to sign in again
after a few weeks. If many articles in the queue show «🔒 no access», sign in and click
«Retry failed».


WHAT YOU CAN PASTE
------------------
  a DOI                      10.1007/s12393-026-09447-8
  a link to an article       https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/...
  a whole reference list     the DOIs are found automatically
  a search by title          title: Article title || 2017 || Journal || #24
  files                      .txt .csv .xlsx .docx .ris .bib

The «inbox» folder is your library of DOI lists. Put your own files (txt, csv, xlsx,
docx, ris, bib) there too: they appear in the «⬇ Download articles» window. An old list
is moved with the «🗑» button to «inbox\_архив» (not deleted for good).


BROWSER
-------
The program uses Yandex Browser if it is installed (with it the Elsevier check lasts
longest), otherwise Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome. The downloader's browser has its own
profile and does not touch your normal browser. The profile is kept on this computer
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\ArticleDownloader), not in this folder, so your sign-ins do not travel
with the folder if you copy it to someone.


GO EASY ON THE SUBSCRIPTION
---------------------------
The program pauses between articles (10 s) and downloads at most 300 articles a day. Both
numbers can be changed in «⚙ Settings». Do not remove the pause completely: publishers
block access for the whole university for downloading too often.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
• The «🛟 Report a problem» button at the top: answers to common questions and, if they
  do not help, a short description and the program's log go to the author (via GitHub or
  as copied text). Keys, passwords and the Windows user name are left out of the report.
• The window did not open: run scripts\debug_start.bat, it shows the error.
• The browser hangs: «⚙ Settings» → «Restart the downloader's browser».
• The program's logs are in the «logs» folder.


UPDATES
-------
The blue square next to the program name is your version. At every start the program
checks for a new one; if it is out, a red square with its number lights up next to it.
Click it, then «⬇ Update automatically»: the program downloads the new version, replaces
its own files and opens again. Lists in inbox, downloaded PDFs and settings stay.
By hand: «Exit», close the downloader's browser window, unpack the new archive into the
same folder replacing the files. If the new version starts while the old one is still
running, the new one closes the old one.
An old folder cannot be deleted? Click «Exit» in the program and close the downloader's
browser window; if that does not help — Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) → «Python» → End task.
